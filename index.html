🐧 GuguOS Sawo

A Debian-based operating system designed to be lightweight with Steam Big Picture integration for an optimized gaming experience.

Caution
⚠️ GuguOS is NOT recommended for dual-boot systems and is NOT beginner-friendly. All risks are your responsibility.
What's New (v1.0.1 Alpha)
  • Window Manager: i3 for superior performance.
  • GuguLauncher: Custom fast-access (Python 3).
  • Steam Integration: Direct boot to Steam Big Picture.
  • Low RAM Usage: Minimal resource consumption.
  • Base System: Debian 13 Trixie (antiX base).
